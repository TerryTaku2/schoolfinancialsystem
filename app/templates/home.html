Everything the school office needs

One system for the bursar, the head, teachers, the librarian and parents. Each person sees only what their role allows.

Fees & receipts

Fee structures per class, term invoices in one click, receipts for cash, bank and mobile money, scholarships, statements and debtors lists.

USD, ZiG and more

Every amount recorded in the currency it was paid in, never converted. Daily rates give combined totals; rand, pula and others can be added.

Payroll

PAYE on ZIMRA tax tables, AIDS levy, NSSA, WCIF and ZIMDEF, salaries split across currencies, payslips and P2 returns.

Proper accounts

Double-entry books kept automatically: income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, general ledger and period locks.

Bank reconciliation

Enter the bank statement, match it to the books, post charges and interest, and see exactly what is outstanding.

Students & academics

Admissions, classes, timetable, attendance, marks, report cards and end-of-year promotion, following the Zimbabwe structure.

Library

Catalogue, issue and return at the desk, textbooks for the whole class, overdue lists, and fines added to the fees account.

Asset register

Buildings, vehicles, computers and furniture with monthly depreciation posted straight to the accounts.

Works the way schools work

No installation and no special computer: open it in a browser, or install it on a phone or laptop like any app.
